# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

VisBal ("us", "we", or "our") operates the Duck Coin Planner mobile application (the "App").

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, where it is kept, which third-party services are involved, and the choices you have.

## 1. Your Data Stays on Your Device

**We do not collect, store, or receive your personal, financial, or trip information on our own servers.** The App has no user accounts and does not require you to sign in.

The following information is stored only on your device:

* Income and spending entries, including amounts, dates, and notes
* Trip logs, including start and destination places, distances, odometer readings, trip types, and notes
* Receipt photos
* App settings, such as your chosen language and currency

Because this information stays on your device, we cannot see, access, or recover it.

If your device's own backup feature is switched on (for example, Android Backup to your Google account), your device may include the App's data in that backup. This is controlled by your device settings, not by us.

## 2. Camera and Photos

The App lets you take a photo of a receipt with your camera or choose an existing image from your device.

* **Purpose:** Photos are used only to read the total and date from your receipt and to keep a copy of the receipt with your spending entry.
* **On-device text recognition:** The App uses Google ML Kit to read text from receipts. The image and the text read from it are processed on your device and are not sent to us or to Google. Like other Google ML Kit features, it may send Google limited technical information, such as device and app details and usage and performance statistics, to keep the feature working and improve it.
* **Where receipts are saved:** Receipt images are saved in the App's private storage on your device and in your device's photo gallery, in albums named "Duck Coin Receipts" followed by the month (for example, "Duck Coin Receipts 2026-10").

## 3. Advertising

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. We do not collect your information for advertising, but Google AdMob may automatically collect and use certain information to show, measure, and improve ads, including:

* Your device's advertising ID
* Device information, such as manufacturer, model, and operating system version
* IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
* Information about how you interact with ads

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

### Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tools. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time by opening the App's menu and selecting **Ad privacy choices**.

To learn how Google uses information from apps that show its ads, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Google's full privacy policy is available at [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Choices for US Residents

If you live in a US state with applicable privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for personalised ads. Open the App's menu and select **Ad privacy choices**.

## 4. Internet Use

The App uses your internet connection only to:

* Load and show ads through Google AdMob
* Download font files from Google Fonts the first time you create a PDF report (they are then kept on your device)
* Open this Privacy Policy in your web browser when you tap the link in the App

None of your entries, trips, notes, or receipts are uploaded during these connections. As with any internet request, the services involved (such as Google) can see your device's IP address.

## 5. Backups, Reports, and Exports

You can create a backup of your data, PDF reports, and trip logs (PDF or CSV).

* **Your choice:** Backups, reports, and exports are created only when you choose to make them.
* **Where they go:** You decide where to save or share them, such as your device storage, personal cloud storage, email, or a messaging app. We do not receive, host, or have access to these files.
* **What a backup contains:** A backup file includes your entries, trips, receipt photos, and settings. Backup files are not encrypted, so please keep them somewhere safe.

## 6. Deleting Your Data

* **Single entries or trips:** Delete them in the App at any time by swiping them away.
* **All App data:** Uninstall the App. This removes all entries, trips, notes, settings, and the App's private copies of receipts from your device.
* **Files outside the App:** Receipt photos in your gallery's "Duck Coin Receipts" albums, and any backups, reports, or exports you saved or shared, are not removed when you uninstall the App. You can delete them using your device's gallery or file manager, or wherever you saved or shared them.

## 7. Security

Because your data is stored on your device, its safety depends on your device's own security. We recommend using a screen lock and keeping your device's software up to date.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at or designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the information you enter stays on your device and is never sent to us, we do not collect, track, or keep any children's data.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add new features. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
